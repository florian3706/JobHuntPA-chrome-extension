Connect this browser to your JobHuntPA

The extension sends pages only to your own server. To connect it:

  1. In JobHuntPA, open Settings, then Browser extension, and press Connect a browser extension.
  2. Copy the server address and the key it shows (the key is shown once).
  3. Paste both on this extension's Options page and press Connect.

Once connected, the popup follows the theme chosen in your JobHuntPA account.
